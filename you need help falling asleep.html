If you're still awake, and really want to go to sleep: here's a trick that ALWAYS works for me. And need I remind you, you're talking to the expert here. 😬

Ok, make the room completely dark. Draw the blackout curtains. No night lights. Pitch dark. After that, you wanna do nothing but stare into the darkness! Sounds silly, I know, but try it. Don't look at an object, look at blackness in the room. Your eyes might try to close. Don't let them. Until they finally do.
